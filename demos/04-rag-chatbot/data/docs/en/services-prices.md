# Services and prices

Prices include VAT where applicable (most dental treatments are VAT-exempt in Spain). They are guide prices for patients without insurance: you get a final written quote after your first visit, and it never changes without your approval.

## First visit and diagnosis

- First visit with full check-up and diagnosis: free.
- Panoramic X-ray: 30 EUR.
- Intraoral (periapical) X-rays: 12 EUR each.
- 3D dental CT scan (CBCT), only when the dentist recommends it: 80 EUR.

## Hygiene and prevention

- Teeth cleaning (professional dental hygiene with ultrasonic scaling and polishing): 50 EUR. It takes about 40 minutes.
- Deep cleaning per quadrant (scaling and root planing for inflamed gums): 70 EUR per quadrant.
- Fissure sealants for children: 25 EUR per tooth.
- Fluoride application: 15 EUR.

## General treatments

- Filling (composite): 60 EUR to 90 EUR depending on size.
- Root canal treatment: 180 EUR to 300 EUR depending on the number of canals.
- Simple tooth extraction: 60 EUR.
- Wisdom tooth extraction: 120 EUR to 180 EUR.
- Night guard for bruxism (teeth grinding): 250 EUR.

## Cosmetic dentistry

- In-clinic teeth whitening: 290 EUR.
- Combined whitening (in-clinic plus take-home trays): 350 EUR.
- Composite veneer: 150 EUR per tooth.
- Porcelain veneer: 450 EUR per tooth.

## Orthodontics

- Orthodontic assessment (photos, records and treatment plan): 60 EUR, deducted if you start treatment.
- Metal braces: from 1,800 EUR for the full treatment.
- Invisible orthodontics with clear aligners: from 2,900 EUR for the full treatment.
- Fixed or removable retainer at the end: included in the treatment price.

## Implants and prosthetics

- Titanium dental implant (surgery and implant only): from 950 EUR.
- Implant with porcelain crown: from 1,450 EUR.
- Zirconia crown on a natural tooth: 420 EUR.
- Full removable denture: from 650 EUR.

## Children's dentistry

- Check-up for children under 14: free.
- Filling on a baby tooth: 45 EUR.

## Treatments we do not offer

We do not perform major maxillofacial surgery, treatments under general sedation, or facial aesthetic medicine (for example Botox or fillers). In those cases we refer you to a specialist centre.
