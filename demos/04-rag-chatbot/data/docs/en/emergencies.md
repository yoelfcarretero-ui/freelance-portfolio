# Emergency policy

## What counts as a dental emergency

We treat as a dental emergency: severe toothache that painkillers do not relieve, an infection or abscess with a swollen face, a tooth broken or knocked out by a blow, bleeding that does not stop after an extraction, or a broken denture or brace that is hurting you.

## Severe pain during opening hours

Call +34 910 000 000. We keep urgent slots free every day and will see you the same day. An emergency consultation costs 40 EUR; if treatment is done in that visit, the fee is deducted from the treatment price.

## Outside opening hours

Clinic patients can call the emergency line +34 600 000 000 on Saturday afternoons and Sundays from 10:00 to 20:00. An on-call dentist assesses the case by phone and, if needed, books you in as soon as possible.

## When to go to hospital or call 112

Call 112 or go to a hospital emergency department if you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, facial swelling that spreads quickly or reaches the eye or neck, a high fever together with tooth pain, or a hard blow to the face or jaw. These cases cannot be treated at a dental clinic.

## On your way to the clinic

- If a whole tooth has been knocked out, hold it by the crown (not the root), do not scrub it, and keep it in milk or in your saliva. Come straight away: the first hours are critical.
- For pain, you can take the painkiller you normally use, following the leaflet. Do not apply heat to a swollen area.
- This assistant cannot diagnose: if in doubt, call the clinic or 112.
