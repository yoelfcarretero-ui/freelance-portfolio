# Insurance and payment

## Insurers we work with

We are an in-network clinic for Adeslas, Sanitas, DKV and Asisa. If you have a dental policy with one of these insurers, bring your insurance card to your appointment: we apply your policy's cover and rates directly.

If your insurer is not on the list, you can be treated as a private patient and claim a refund from your insurer. We give you a detailed invoice to submit.

## Payment methods

- Cash.
- Debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard and American Express).
- Bizum.
- Bank transfer for treatments with an accepted quote.

We do not accept cheques.

## When you pay

Single-session treatments are paid at the end of the appointment. For long treatments (orthodontics, implants) you pay a deposit at the start and the rest in instalments according to the accepted quote.

## Financing

For treatments over 300 EUR we offer interest-free financing for up to 12 months, subject to approval by the finance company. Longer terms, up to 36 months, carry interest, which we explain before you sign. You need a Spanish ID (DNI or NIE) and a Spanish bank account.

## Invoices

We issue an invoice for every payment. Ask at reception or by email at hola@clinicasonrisa.example.

## Discounts

- 10% off your second cleaning of the year.
- 5% off treatments over 1,000 EUR paid in a single payment.
- Discounts cannot be combined with insurer rates.
