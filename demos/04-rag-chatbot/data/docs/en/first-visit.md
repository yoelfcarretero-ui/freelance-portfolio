# First visit FAQ

## How much does the first visit cost?

The first visit is free and includes a full check-up and diagnosis. If the dentist needs a panoramic X-ray for the diagnosis, it costs 30 EUR.

## How long does it take?

The first visit takes about 45 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes early to fill in the patient form.

## What should I bring?

- ID card or passport.
- Your dental insurance card, if you have one.
- A list of the medicines you take and any allergies.
- Recent dental X-rays or reports, if you have them.

## What happens at the first visit?

The dentist checks your teeth, gums and bite, explains what they found and, if you need treatment, we give you a written quote. We never start a treatment without your approval.

## Do you treat children?

Yes. We see children from the age of 3. The check-up for children under 14 is free. Minors must come with a parent or legal guardian.

## Do you speak English?

Yes. Several members of the team speak fluent English. Mention it when you book and we will assign an English-speaking dentist.

## I am afraid of the dentist

That is very common. Tell us when you book: we schedule extra time, explain each step before doing it and can stop whenever you need. We do not offer general sedation.

## Pregnancy

If you are pregnant, let us know before your appointment. Check-ups and cleanings can be done as usual; X-rays and some treatments are postponed unless they are urgent.
