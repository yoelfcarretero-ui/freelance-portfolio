# Opening hours and location

## Opening hours

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 to 20:00, open through lunchtime.
- Saturdays: we are open on Saturday mornings, 10:00 to 14:00 (appointment only).
- Sundays and national or Madrid regional public holidays: closed.

## Summer hours

From 1 to 31 August we are open Monday to Thursday, 9:00 to 15:00. The clinic is closed on Fridays and Saturdays in August. Patient emergencies in August are handled under the emergency policy.

## Address

Smile Dental Clinic (Clínica Dental Sonrisa) is at Calle de la Sonrisa 12, ground floor, 28004 Madrid (Malasaña neighbourhood).

## Getting here

- Metro: Tribunal station (lines 1 and 10), a 4-minute walk.
- Bus: lines 3, 40 and 149, stop on Calle Fuencarral.
- Car: there is a public car park 200 metres away. The clinic has no parking of its own. The area is inside Madrid Central (low-emission zone).

## Accessibility

The clinic is at street level with no steps and has an accessible toilet. All treatment rooms are wheelchair accessible.
