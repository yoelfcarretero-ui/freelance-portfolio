# Booking and cancellations

## How to book an appointment

You can book in three ways:

- By phone on +34 910 000 000, during opening hours.
- By WhatsApp on +34 600 000 000.
- With the contact form on the website or from this assistant: leave your name, email and what you need, and reception will contact you within 24 working hours to give you a time.

We usually have appointments available within 2 to 5 working days. Emergencies are seen the same day.

## Confirmations and reminders

You will receive a confirmation by SMS or WhatsApp when you book, and a reminder 48 hours before the appointment.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

You can cancel or change your appointment free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice, by phone or WhatsApp.

## Missed appointments and late notice

If you miss two appointments without giving 24 hours' notice, we will ask for a 20 EUR deposit for your next booking, which is deducted from the treatment.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move your appointment to another day so other patients are not delayed.

## Cancellations by the clinic

If the clinic needs to change your appointment, we will let you know as soon as possible and give you priority for the new date.
